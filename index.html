①

开头引入

1.With the rapid development of society and economy, ... has become a heated topic among the public.

随着社会与经济的快速发展，……已成为公众热议的话题。

句式：with 复合结构作状语　模板：With the rapid development of ______, ______ has become a heated topic among the public.

2.Nowadays, there is a growing tendency that more and more people..., which has aroused wide public concern.

如今，越来越多人……的趋势日益明显，这已引起公众的广泛关注。

句式：there be 句型 + that 同位语从句 + which 非限定从句　模板：Nowadays, there is a growing tendency that more and more people ______, which has aroused wide public concern.

3.When it comes to..., people's opinions vary from person to person.

谈到……，人们的看法因人而异。

句式：when it comes to 引出话题　模板：When it comes to ______, people's opinions vary from person to person.

4.Recently, the issue of...has been brought into focus, drawing much attention from all walks of life.

近来，……问题已被推到风口浪尖，引起各界广泛关注。

句式：现在完成时被动语态 + 现在分词伴随状语　模板：Recently, the issue of ______ has been brought into focus, drawing much attention from all walks of life.

5.It is universally acknowledged that...plays an increasingly important role in our daily life.

人们普遍认为，……在我们的日常生活中发挥着越来越重要的作用。

句式：it 形式主语 + play an important role in　模板：It is universally acknowledged that ______ plays an increasingly important role in our daily life.

②

观点表达

1.From my perspective, the advantages of...far outweigh its disadvantages.

在我看来，……的好处远远大于坏处。

句式：outweigh 一词完成利弊对比　模板：From my perspective, the advantages of ______ far outweigh its disadvantages.

2.As far as I am concerned,...is not only necessary but also beneficial in the long run.

就我而言，……不仅必要，而且从长远看是有益的。

句式：not only ... but also + in the long run　模板：As far as I am concerned, ______ is not only necessary but also beneficial in the long run.

3.Some people hold the view that..., while others believe the opposite is true.

有些人认为……，另一些人则认为事实恰恰相反。

句式：while 一句呈现双方观点　模板：Some people hold the view that ______, while others believe the opposite is true.

4.I am firmly convinced that..., and I will illustrate my reasons in the following paragraphs.

我坚信……，下文将逐一说明我的理由。

句式：表态 + 全文结构预告　模板：I am firmly convinced that ______, and I will illustrate my reasons in the following paragraphs.

5.Whether we like it or not,...has become an inevitable trend that we have to face.

无论我们愿不愿意，……已成为我们不得不面对的必然趋势。

句式：whether 让步状语 + that 定语从句　模板：Whether we like it or not, ______ has become an inevitable trend that we have to face.

③

因果论证

1.The reasons for this phenomenon are various, among which the most important one is that...

这一现象的原因多种多样，其中最重要的一点是……

句式：among which 介词提前的定语从句　模板：The reasons for this phenomenon are various, among which the most important one is that ______.

2.As a result of..., an increasing number of people have begun to realize the importance of...

由于……，越来越多的人开始意识到……的重要性。

句式：as a result of 介词短语 + an increasing number of　模板：As a result of ______, an increasing number of people have begun to realize the importance of ______.

3.Consequently,..., which in turn affects...deeply.

因此，……，而这又反过来深刻影响着……

句式：Consequently 衔接结果 + which in turn 连锁影响　模板：Consequently, ______, which in turn affects ______ deeply.

4.Owing to the joint efforts of the government and the public,...has been greatly improved in recent years.

由于政府和公众的共同努力，近年来……已得到极大改善。

句式：owing to 表原因 + 现在完成时被动　模板：Owing to the joint efforts of ______ and ______, ______ has been greatly improved in recent years.

5.This is mainly because..., and therefore it exerts a profound influence on...

这主要是因为……，因此它对……产生了深远的影响。

句式：exert a profound influence on 高级搭配　模板：This is mainly because ______, and therefore it exerts a profound influence on ______.

← 上一类：观点表达下一类：对比转折 →
④

对比转折

1.On the one hand,...; on the other hand,...

一方面，……；另一方面，……

句式：经典两面论证框架，利弊题通用　模板：On the one hand, ______; on the other hand, ______.

2.Despite the obvious benefits mentioned above, we should not ignore the potential problems it may cause.

尽管有上述明显好处，我们也不应忽视它可能带来的潜在问题。

句式：despite 介词转折 + 定语从句点出隐患　模板：Despite the obvious benefits mentioned above, we should not ignore the potential problems it may cause.

3.While technology brings us great convenience, it also quietly changes the way we think and behave.

科技在给我们带来极大便利的同时，也在悄然改变我们思考和行事的方式。

句式：while 单句内对比 + 省略 that 的定语从句　模板：While ______ brings us great convenience, it also quietly changes the way we ______.

4.In contrast, those who...tend to..., which shows a completely different picture.

相比之下，那些……的人往往……，这呈现出完全不同的景象。

句式：in contrast 转折 + which 从句总结对比结果　模板：In contrast, those who ______ tend to ______, which shows a completely different picture.

5.However, every coin has two sides, and...is no exception.

然而，凡事都有两面性，……也不例外。

句式：谚语 + no exception，利转弊的万能过渡　模板：However, every coin has two sides, and ______ is no exception.

← 上一类：因果论证下一类：建议措施 →
⑤

建议措施

1.It is high time that effective measures were taken to solve this serious problem.

该采取有效措施来解决这一严重问题了。

句式：it is high time + 虚拟语气（were taken）　模板：It is high time that effective measures were taken to solve ______.

2.The government and relevant departments should make joint efforts to create a better environment for...

政府和相关部门应共同努力，为……营造更好的环境。

句式：宏观主体 + make joint efforts 搭配　模板：The government and relevant departments should make joint efforts to create a better environment for ______.

3.Only in this way can we solve the problem fundamentally and avoid similar issues in the future.

只有这样，我们才能从根本上解决问题，并在未来避免类似问题。

句式：Only in this way 引起倒装，措施段收尾首选　模板：Only in this way can we solve ______ fundamentally and avoid ______ in the future.

4.As college students, we are supposed to start from the small things around us instead of just talking.

作为大学生，我们应该从身边小事做起，而不是只停留在口头上。

句式：be supposed to 替换 should + instead of 对比　模板：As ______, we are supposed to start from ______ instead of just talking.

5.In addition, raising public awareness through education is also an effective way to tackle this issue.

此外，通过教育提高公众意识，也是解决这一问题的有效途径。

句式：动名词作主语 + tackle 高级动词替换 solve　模板：In addition, raising public awareness through ______ is also an effective way to tackle this issue.

← 上一类：对比转折下一类：结尾总结 →
⑥

结尾总结

1.In conclusion, although...has some drawbacks, its advantages are far more significant.

总之，尽管……有一些缺点，但其优势要显著得多。

句式：although 让步 + 总结，利弊型议论文标准结尾　模板：In conclusion, although ______ has some drawbacks, its advantages are far more significant.

2.To sum up,...is a trend that cannot be reversed, and what we should do is adapt to it wisely.

总而言之，……是不可逆转的趋势，我们要做的就是明智地适应它。

句式：cannot be reversed 被动 + what 主语从句　模板：To sum up, ______ is a trend that cannot be reversed, and what we should do is adapt to it wisely.

3.Taking all these factors into consideration, we may safely come to the conclusion that...

综合考虑以上因素，我们可以有把握地得出这样的结论：……

句式：分词状语开头 + come to the conclusion that　模板：Taking all these factors into consideration, we may safely come to the conclusion that ______.

4.In a word, only when both individuals and society make efforts can we expect a brighter future.

总之，只有个人和社会共同努力，我们才能期待更光明的未来。

句式：only when 倒装 + 双主体升华　模板：In a word, only when both ______ and ______ make efforts can we expect a brighter future.

5.Therefore, it is reasonable for us to believe that...will play a more important role in the years to come.

因此，我们有理由相信，……在未来几年将发挥更重要的作用。

句式：it is reasonable to believe 表态 + in the years to come　模板：Therefore, it is reasonable for us to believe that ______ will play a more important role in the years to come.

教育学习

核心好词 10

  • acquire knowledge获取知识
  • lay a solid foundation for为……打下坚实基础
  • broaden one's horizons开阔眼界
  • cultivate critical thinking培养批判性思维
  • stimulate one's interest in激发对……的兴趣
  • keep pace with the times与时俱进
  • make the most of充分利用
  • a sense of achievement成就感
  • be equipped with具备、配备有
  • play a vital role in在……中起关键作用

高分好句 5

1.It is undeniable that education plays an indispensable role in shaping our future, which further determines the development of the whole society.

不可否认，教育在塑造我们的未来方面起着不可或缺的作用，而这又进一步决定着整个社会的发展。

议论文开头句式：which 非限定性定语从句（指代前面整句）　模板：It is undeniable that ______ plays an indispensable role in ______, which further ______.

2.Only by combining classroom learning with social practice can students truly master what they have learned.

只有把课堂学习与社会实践结合起来，学生才能真正掌握所学的内容。

措施段句式：Only + 方式状语前置，主句部分倒装　模板：Only by ______ can ______ truly ______ what ______.

3.It is the teachers' constant encouragement that motivates students to overcome various difficulties in their studies.

正是老师不断的鼓励，激励学生克服学习中的各种困难。

师生关系句式：It is ... that 强调句（强调主语）　模板：It is ______ that motivates ______ to ______.

4.Facing fierce competition in the job market, more and more college students choose to further their education after graduation.

面对就业市场的激烈竞争，越来越多的大学生选择毕业后继续深造。

现象解释句式：现在分词短语作状语　模板：Facing ______, more and more ______ choose to ______ after graduation.

5.There is no denying that students who develop good study habits at an early stage tend to achieve better academic performance.

无可否认，早早养成良好学习习惯的学生往往能取得更好的学业成绩。

观点表达句式：There is no denying that + who 定语从句 + tend to　模板：There is no denying that ______ who ______ tend to ______.

科技网络

核心好词 10

  • with the rapid development of随着……的快速发展
  • bring great convenience to为……带来极大便利
  • a double-edged sword双刃剑
  • be addicted to沉迷于
  • face-to-face communication面对面交流
  • keep in touch with与……保持联系
  • at an amazing speed以惊人的速度
  • pose a threat to对……构成威胁
  • improve efficiency提高效率
  • the information age信息时代

高分好句 5

1.Not only does the Internet provide us with abundant information, but it also changes the way we live and work.

互联网不仅为我们提供了丰富的信息，还改变了我们生活和工作的方式。

网络正面句式：Not only 句首倒装 + but also 并列　模板：Not only does ______ provide us with ______, but it also changes ______.

2.Equipped with smartphones, people can now handle almost everything online without leaving their homes.

有了智能手机，人们如今足不出户就能在网上处理几乎所有事情。

移动生活句式：过去分词短语作状语　模板：Equipped with ______, people can now ______ without leaving their homes.

3.It is modern technology that makes it possible for us to communicate with friends far away in a few seconds.

正是现代科技使我们能在几秒钟内与远方的朋友交流。

科技作用句式：强调句 + it 作形式宾语　模板：It is ______ that makes it possible for us to ______ in a few seconds.

4.The Internet, which has become an essential part of our daily life, brings both opportunities and challenges to young people.

互联网已成为我们日常生活中不可或缺的一部分，它给年轻人带来了机遇，也带来了挑战。

两面性句式：插入式非限定定语从句　模板：______, which has become an essential part of ______, brings both ______ and ______ to ______.

5.With the popularity of mobile payment, spending money has become easier than ever before, which may lead to over-consumption.

随着移动支付的普及，花钱变得比以往任何时候都容易，而这可能导致过度消费。

利弊分析句式：with 复合结构 + which 非限定从句　模板：With the ______ of ______, ______ has become easier than ever before, which may lead to ______.

环境保护

核心好词 10

  • environmental protection环境保护
  • raise people's awareness of提高人们的……意识
  • a low-carbon lifestyle低碳生活方式
  • do harm to对……有害
  • take effective measures采取有效措施
  • sustainable development可持续发展
  • run out of耗尽、用完
  • make joint efforts共同努力
  • reduce pollution减少污染
  • precious natural resources宝贵的自然资源

高分好句 5

1.Only when everyone takes responsibility for the environment can we expect a greener and more beautiful world.

只有每个人都对环境负起责任，我们才能期待一个更绿色、更美好的世界。

问题解决结尾句式：Only when 状语从句前置，主句倒装　模板：Only when ______ can we expect a ______ world.

2.It is high time that we took effective measures to protect our environment from further damage.

我们该采取有效措施，保护环境免遭进一步破坏。

建议措施句式：It is high time that + 过去式（虚拟语气）　模板：It is high time that we took effective measures to ______.

3.Environmental problems, which seem far away from our daily life, actually affect every one of us in a direct way.

环境问题看似离我们的日常生活很遥远，实际上却直接影响着我们每一个人。

话题引入句式：插入式定语从句 + actually 转折　模板：______, which seem far away from ______, actually affect ______ in a direct way.

4.Living a low-carbon lifestyle, such as taking public transport, can greatly reduce the pollution caused by cars.

践行低碳生活方式，比如乘坐公共交通，能大大减少汽车造成的污染。

措施举例句式：动名词短语作主语 + 过去分词后置定语　模板：______, such as ______, can greatly reduce the ______ caused by ______.

5.There is no denying that protecting the environment is not the duty of the government alone but of every single citizen.

无可否认，保护环境不只是政府的职责，更是每一个公民的职责。

观点表达句式：not ... but 并列结构　模板：There is no denying that ______ is not the duty of ______ alone but of ______.

社会现象

核心好词 10

  • a heated discussion热议
  • attract widespread attention引起广泛关注
  • social phenomenon社会现象
  • give rise to引起、导致
  • to some extent在某种程度上
  • have a positive influence on对……产生积极影响
  • reflect the reality反映现实
  • cause-and-effect relationship因果关系
  • draw a conclusion from从……中得出结论
  • take...into consideration把……考虑在内

高分好句 5

1.It is quite common nowadays that young people would rather order food online than cook by themselves.

如今，年轻人宁愿点外卖也不愿自己做饭，这已相当普遍。

现象开头句式：It 形式主语 + would rather ... than 结构　模板：It is quite common nowadays that ______ would rather ______ than ______.

2.This phenomenon, which has aroused heated discussion among the public, reflects the great changes in our lifestyle.

这一现象引起了公众的热烈讨论，它反映出我们生活方式的巨大变化。

现象评价句式：插入式定语从句　模板：This phenomenon, which has aroused heated discussion among the public, reflects ______.

3.It is not the phenomenon itself but the reasons behind it that deserve our careful analysis.

值得仔细分析的，不是现象本身，而是现象背后的原因。

原因总起句式：It is not A but B that ... 强调句的否定并列形式　模板：It is not ______ but ______ that deserve our careful analysis.

4.While some people regard it as a symbol of social progress, others argue that it may bring potential problems.

有些人把它视为社会进步的标志，另一些人则认为它可能带来潜在问题。

观点对比句式：while 引导对比状语从句　模板：While some people regard ______ as ______, others argue that ______.

5.Judging from the current social situation, this trend is very likely to continue and even deepen in the foreseeable future.

从当前的社会形势判断，这一趋势很可能持续下去，并在可预见的未来进一步加深。

趋势预测句式：judging from 独立成分 + be likely to　模板：Judging from ______, ______ is very likely to continue and even deepen in the foreseeable future.

个人成长

核心好词 10

  • overcome difficulties克服困难
  • build up one's confidence建立自信
  • step out of one's comfort zone走出舒适区
  • learn from failure从失败中学习
  • develop a good habit养成好习惯
  • set a clear goal设定明确目标
  • self-discipline自律
  • realize one's potential发挥潜能
  • be faced with面对
  • personal growth个人成长

高分好句 5

1.It is failure rather than success that teaches us the most valuable lessons in our personal growth.

在个人成长中，教给我们最宝贵经验的不是成功，而是失败。

观点句句式：强调句 + rather than 对比　模板：It is ______ rather than ______ that teaches us ______.

2.Never before have young people had so many opportunities to develop themselves and realize their dreams.

年轻人从未像今天这样，拥有如此多的机会去发展自我、实现梦想。

时代机遇句式：Never before 前置引起完全倒装　模板：Never before have ______ had so many opportunities to ______ and ______.

3.Those who dare to step out of their comfort zone are more likely to discover their true potential.

敢于走出舒适区的人，更有可能发现自己的真正潜力。

勇气成长句式：who 定语从句作主语 + be likely to　模板：Those who dare to ______ are more likely to ______.

4.Setting clear goals at the beginning of each semester helps students make steady progress in their studies.

在每学期开始时设定明确的目标，有助于学生在学业上稳步前进。

建议措施句式：动名词短语作主语　模板：______ at the beginning of ______ helps ______ make steady progress in ______.

5.Only through constant practice and reflection can we turn what we have learned into real ability.

只有通过不断的练习和反思，我们才能把学到的东西转化为真正的能力。

总结升华句式：Only + 介词短语倒装 + what 宾语从句　模板：Only through ______ can we turn what we have learned into ______.

生活方式

核心好词 10

  • lead a healthy lifestyle过健康的生活
  • keep a balanced diet保持均衡饮食
  • take regular exercise经常锻炼
  • stay up late熬夜
  • work-life balance工作与生活的平衡
  • relieve stress缓解压力
  • be beneficial to对……有益
  • physical and mental health身心健康
  • cut down on减少、削减
  • get rid of摆脱、戒除

高分好句 5

1.Not only does regular exercise keep us physically healthy, but it also helps us relieve mental stress effectively.

规律锻炼不仅能让我们身体健康，还能有效帮我们缓解心理压力。

锻炼好处句式：Not only 倒装 + 平行结构　模板：Not only does ______ keep us ______, but it also helps us ______.

2.It is a balanced diet and enough sleep that lay a solid foundation for a healthy lifestyle.

正是均衡的饮食和充足的睡眠，为健康的生活方式打下坚实基础。

健康观点句式：强调句（强调并列主语）　模板：It is ______ and ______ that lay a solid foundation for ______.

3.People who stay up late playing with their phones often suffer from poor memory and low efficiency the next day.

熬夜玩手机的人，常常第二天记忆力变差、效率低下。

习惯危害句式：who 定语从句 + 现在分词伴随状语　模板：People who ______ often suffer from ______.

4.Spending too much time on short videos, many young people find it hard to concentrate on their studies or work.

在短视频上花太多时间，许多年轻人发现自己很难专注于学习或工作。

短视频现象句式：现在分词作状语 + it 形式宾语　模板：______ too much time on ______, many ______ find it hard to ______.

5.It is advisable for us to schedule our time properly so that we can achieve a balance between study and rest.

我们最好合理安排时间，以便在学习和休息之间取得平衡。

时间安排句式：It is advisable for sb to do + so that 目的状语从句　模板：It is advisable for us to ______ so that we can achieve a balance between ______ and ______.

职业发展

核心好词 10

  • hunt for a job求职
  • be qualified for胜任、有资格
  • plan one's career做职业规划
  • a sense of responsibility责任感
  • team spirit团队精神
  • gain practical experience获得实践经验
  • be engaged in从事于
  • seize the opportunity抓住机会
  • interpersonal skills人际交往能力
  • realize one's career ambition实现职业理想

高分好句 5

1.Participating in internships before graduation enables students to gain practical experience and adapt to the workplace quickly.

毕业前参加实习，能让学生获得实践经验并迅速适应职场。

实习价值句式：动名词作主语 + enable sb to do　模板：______ before graduation enables students to ______ and ______.

2.Employers today value those who not only have professional knowledge but also possess good interpersonal skills.

如今用人单位看重的人，既要有专业知识，也要有良好的人际交往能力。

用人要求句式：who 定语从句 + not only ... but also　模板：Employers today value those who not only ______ but also ______.

3.It is a clear career plan that helps graduates avoid being lost in the crowded job market.

正是清晰的职业规划，帮助毕业生避免在拥挤的就业市场中迷失方向。

职业规划句式：强调句 + avoid doing　模板：It is ______ that helps ______ avoid ______.

4.Only by improving ourselves constantly can we stand out in the fierce competition of the job market.

只有不断提升自己，我们才能在激烈的就业竞争中脱颖而出。

竞争力句式：Only by doing 倒装　模板：Only by ______ can we stand out in ______.

5.While a well-paid job is attractive, job satisfaction should never be ignored when we are making a career choice.

高薪工作固然有吸引力，但在做职业选择时，工作满意度绝不应被忽视。

择业观句式：while 让步对比 + 被动语态　模板：While ______ is attractive, ______ should never be ignored when ______.
