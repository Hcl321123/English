01

教育学习

核心好词 10

  • acquire knowledge获取知识
  • lay a solid foundation for为……打下坚实基础
  • broaden one's horizons开阔眼界
  • cultivate critical thinking培养批判性思维
  • stimulate one's interest in激发对……的兴趣
  • keep pace with the times与时俱进
  • make the most of充分利用
  • a sense of achievement成就感
  • be equipped with具备、配备有
  • play a vital role in在……中起关键作用

高分好句 5

1.It is undeniable that education plays an indispensable role in shaping our future, which further determines the development of the whole society.

适用场景：议论文开头，强调教育重要性 · 句式：which 非限定定语从句

2.Only by combining classroom learning with social practice can students truly master what they have learned.

适用场景：问题解决型作文的措施段 · 句式：Only 倒装

3.It is the teachers' constant encouragement that motivates students to overcome various difficulties in their studies.

适用场景：写师生关系、教育方法 · 句式：It is...that 强调句

4.Facing fierce competition in the job market, more and more college students choose to further their education after graduation.

适用场景：现象解释型作文，谈考研热、终身学习 · 句式：现在分词作状语

5.There is no denying that students who develop good study habits at an early stage tend to achieve better academic performance.

适用场景：议论文观点表达 · 句式：who 引导的定语从句

02

科技网络

核心好词 10

  • with the rapid development of随着……的快速发展
  • bring great convenience to为……带来极大便利
  • a double-edged sword双刃剑
  • be addicted to沉迷于
  • face-to-face communication面对面交流
  • keep in touch with与……保持联系
  • at an amazing speed以惊人的速度
  • pose a threat to对……构成威胁
  • improve efficiency提高效率
  • the information age信息时代

高分好句 5

1.Not only does the Internet provide us with abundant information, but it also changes the way we live and work.

适用场景：谈网络利弊的正面段 · 句式：Not only 倒装

2.Equipped with smartphones, people can now handle almost everything online without leaving their homes.

适用场景：现象解释型作文，写移动支付、网购 · 句式：过去分词作状语

3.It is modern technology that makes it possible for us to communicate with friends far away in a few seconds.

适用场景：强调科技的作用 · 句式：强调句 + it 作形式宾语

4.The Internet, which has become an essential part of our daily life, brings both opportunities and challenges to young people.

适用场景：议论文中间段，引出两面性 · 句式：插入式定语从句

5.With the popularity of mobile payment, spending money has become easier than ever before, which may lead to over-consumption.

适用场景：观点对比、问题分析类作文 · 句式：with 复合结构 + which 从句

03

环境保护

核心好词 10

  • environmental protection环境保护
  • raise people's awareness of提高人们的……意识
  • a low-carbon lifestyle低碳生活方式
  • do harm to对……有害
  • take effective measures采取有效措施
  • sustainable development可持续发展
  • run out of耗尽、用完
  • make joint efforts共同努力
  • reduce pollution减少污染
  • precious natural resources宝贵的自然资源

高分好句 5

1.Only when everyone takes responsibility for the environment can we expect a greener and more beautiful world.

适用场景：问题解决型作文结尾 · 句式：Only when 倒装

2.It is high time that we took effective measures to protect our environment from further damage.

适用场景：建议措施段 · 句式：It is high time 虚拟语气

3.Environmental problems, which seem far away from our daily life, actually affect every one of us in a direct way.

适用场景：议论文开头引出话题 · 句式：which 非限定定语从句

4.Living a low-carbon lifestyle, such as taking public transport, can greatly reduce the pollution caused by cars.

适用场景：建议措施段举例 · 句式：现在分词作主语 + 过去分词后置定语

5.There is no denying that protecting the environment is not the duty of the government alone but of every single citizen.

适用场景：观点表达 · 句式：not...but 并列结构

04

社会现象

核心好词 10

  • a heated discussion热议
  • attract widespread attention引起广泛关注
  • social phenomenon社会现象
  • give rise to引起、导致
  • to some extent在某种程度上
  • have a positive influence on对……产生积极影响
  • reflect the reality反映现实
  • cause-and-effect relationship因果关系
  • draw a conclusion from从……中得出结论
  • take...into consideration把……考虑在内

高分好句 5

1.It is quite common nowadays that young people would rather order food online than cook by themselves.

适用场景：现象解释型作文开头 · 句式：would rather...than 结构

2.This phenomenon, which has aroused heated discussion among the public, reflects the great changes in our lifestyle.

适用场景：引出社会现象后的评价句 · 句式：插入式定语从句

3.It is not the phenomenon itself but the reasons behind it that deserve our careful analysis.

适用场景：分析原因段的总起句 · 句式：强调句的否定并列形式

4.While some people regard it as a symbol of social progress, others argue that it may bring potential problems.

适用场景：观点对比型作文 · 句式：While 引导的对比状语从句

5.Judging from the current social situation, this trend is very likely to continue and even deepen in the foreseeable future.

适用场景：预测趋势段 · 句式：judging from 独立成分 + 不定式结构

个人成长

核心好词 10

  • overcome difficulties克服困难
  • build up one's confidence建立自信
  • step out of one's comfort zone走出舒适区
  • learn from failure从失败中学习
  • develop a good habit养成好习惯
  • set a clear goal设定明确目标
  • self-discipline自律
  • realize one's potential发挥潜能
  • be faced with面对
  • personal growth个人成长

高分好句 5

1.It is failure rather than success that teaches us the most valuable lessons in our personal growth.

适用场景：议论文观点句 · 句式：强调句 + rather than 对比

2.Never before have young people had so many opportunities to develop themselves and realize their dreams.

适用场景：谈时代机遇 · 句式：Never before 否定词前置倒装

3.Those who dare to step out of their comfort zone are more likely to discover their true potential.

适用场景：个人成长、勇气话题 · 句式：who 定语从句作主语

4.Setting clear goals at the beginning of each semester helps students make steady progress in their studies.

适用场景：建议措施段 · 句式：动名词短语作主语

5.Only through constant practice and reflection can we turn what we have learned into real ability.

适用场景：总结段升华 · 句式：Only 倒装 + what 引导的宾语从句

生活方式

核心好词 10

  • lead a healthy lifestyle过健康的生活
  • keep a balanced diet保持均衡饮食
  • take regular exercise经常锻炼
  • stay up late熬夜
  • work-life balance工作与生活的平衡
  • relieve stress缓解压力
  • be beneficial to对……有益
  • physical and mental health身心健康
  • cut down on减少、削减
  • get rid of摆脱、戒除

高分好句 5

1.Not only does regular exercise keep us physically healthy, but it also helps us relieve mental stress effectively.

适用场景：谈锻炼的好处 · 句式：Not only 倒装

2.It is a balanced diet and enough sleep that lay a solid foundation for a healthy lifestyle.

适用场景：健康话题观点句 · 句式：强调句

3.People who stay up late playing with their phones often suffer from poor memory and low efficiency the next day.

适用场景：分析不良生活习惯的危害 · 句式：who 定语从句 + playing 现在分词伴随

4.Spending too much time on short videos, many young people find it hard to concentrate on their studies or work.

适用场景：现象解释型作文，写短视频问题 · 句式：现在分词状语 + it 形式宾语

5.It is advisable for us to schedule our time properly so that we can achieve a balance between study and rest.

适用场景：建议措施段 · 句式：It is advisable + so that 目的状语从句

07

职业发展

核心好词 10

  • hunt for a job求职
  • be qualified for胜任、有资格
  • plan one's career做职业规划
  • a sense of responsibility责任感
  • team spirit团队精神
  • gain practical experience获得实践经验
  • be engaged in从事于
  • seize the opportunity抓住机会
  • interpersonal skills人际交往能力
  • realize one's career ambition实现职业理想

高分好句 5

1.Participating in internships before graduation enables students to gain practical experience and adapt to the workplace quickly.

适用场景：谈实习、实践的作用 · 句式：动名词作主语 + enable sb to do

2.Employers today value those who not only have professional knowledge but also possess good interpersonal skills.

适用场景：谈用人单位要求 · 句式：who 定语从句 + not only...but also

3.It is a clear career plan that helps graduates avoid being lost in the crowded job market.

适用场景：职业规划话题 · 句式：强调句

4.Only by improving ourselves constantly can we stand out in the fierce competition of the job market.

适用场景：问题解决型作文结尾 · 句式：Only 倒装

5.While a well-paid job is attractive, job satisfaction should never be ignored when we are making a career choice.

适用场景：观点对比型作文，谈择业观 · 句式：While 对比 + 被动语态

第二模块 通用功能句型

按写作逻辑提供 6 类万能功能句型，适配绝大多数话题，用于快速搭建全文框架。

① 开头引入 → ⑥ 结尾总结 →
①

开头引入

1.With the rapid development of society and economy, ... has become a heated topic among the public.

句式亮点：with 复合结构作状语，万能引出话题

2.Nowadays, there is a growing tendency that more and more people..., which has aroused wide public concern.

句式亮点：there be 句型 + which 非限定从句补充影响

3.When it comes to..., people's opinions vary from person to person.

句式亮点：when it comes to 引出争议话题，适配观点对比题

4.Recently, the issue of...has been brought into focus, drawing much attention from all walks of life.

句式亮点：现在完成时被动语态 + 现在分词伴随状语

5.It is universally acknowledged that...plays an increasingly important role in our daily life.

句式亮点：it 形式主语 + 高频承认句式，开门见山表态度

↑ 返回模块二下一类：观点表达 →
②

观点表达

1.From my perspective, the advantages of...far outweigh its disadvantages.

句式亮点：outweigh 一词完成利弊对比，简洁有力

2.As far as I am concerned,...is not only necessary but also beneficial in the long run.

句式亮点：not only...but also 并列 + in the long run 时间递进

3.Some people hold the view that..., while others believe the opposite is true.

句式亮点：while 一句话呈现双方观点，观点对比题首选

4.I am firmly convinced that..., and I will illustrate my reasons in the following paragraphs.

句式亮点：表态 + 预告结构，全文框架一目了然

5.Whether we like it or not,...has become an inevitable trend that we have to face.

句式亮点：whether 让步状语 + that 定语从句，语气客观

← 上一类：开头引入下一类：因果论证 →
③

因果论证

1.The reasons for this phenomenon are various, among which the most important one is that...

句式亮点：among which 介词提前的定语从句，引出核心原因

2.As a result of..., an increasing number of people have begun to realize the importance of...

句式亮点：as a result of 介词短语 + an increasing number of 高频替换 many

3.Consequently,..., which in turn affects...deeply.

句式亮点：Consequently 衔接结果 + which in turn 表示连锁影响

4.Owing to the joint efforts of the government and the public,...has been greatly improved in recent years.

句式亮点：owing to 表原因 + 现在完成时呈现变化

5.This is mainly because..., and therefore it exerts a profound influence on...

句式亮点：exert an influence on 高级搭配，替换 have an effect on

← 上一类：观点表达下一类：对比转折 →
④

对比转折

1.On the one hand,...; on the other hand,...

句式亮点：经典两面论证框架，任何利弊题通用

2.Despite the obvious benefits mentioned above, we should not ignore the potential problems it may cause.

句式亮点：despite 介词转折 + 定语从句点出隐患

3.While technology brings us great convenience, it also quietly changes the way we think and behave.

句式亮点：while 单句内对比，含省略 that 的定语从句

4.In contrast, those who...tend to..., which shows a completely different picture.

句式亮点：in contrast 转折 + which 从句总结对比结果

5.However, every coin has two sides, and...is no exception.

句式亮点：谚语 + no exception，利转弊的万能过渡句

← 上一类：因果论证下一类：建议措施 →
⑤

建议措施

1.It is high time that effective measures were taken to solve this serious problem.

句式亮点：it is high time 虚拟语气，经典呼吁句

2.The government and relevant departments should make joint efforts to create a better environment for...

句式亮点：宏观主体 + make joint efforts 搭配

3.Only in this way can we solve the problem fundamentally and avoid similar issues in the future.

句式亮点：Only 状语前置倒装，措施段收尾首选

4.As college students, we are supposed to start from the small things around us instead of just talking.

句式亮点：be supposed to 替换 should + instead of 对比

5.In addition, raising public awareness through education is also an effective way to tackle this issue.

句式亮点：动名词作主语 + tackle 高级动词替换 solve

← 上一类：对比转折下一类：结尾总结 →
⑥

结尾总结

1.In conclusion, although...has some drawbacks, its advantages are far more significant.

句式亮点：although 让步 + 总结，利弊型议论文标准结尾

2.To sum up,...is a trend that cannot be reversed, and what we should do is adapt to it wisely.

句式亮点：cannot be reversed 被动 + what 主语从句，收束有力

3.Taking all these factors into consideration, we may safely come to the conclusion that...

句式亮点：分词状语开头 + safely come to the conclusion 高级表达

4.In a word, only when both individuals and society make efforts can we expect a brighter future.

句式亮点：only when 倒装 + individuals and society 双主体升华

5.Therefore, it is reasonable for us to believe that...will play a more important role in the years to come.

句式亮点：it is reasonable to believe 表态 + in the years to come 展望未来
